## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines physical fitness tracking through the lens of mindful restoration and sustainable self-care. Departing from high-adrenaline, dark-mode, neon-accented workout apps that incite anxiety and pressure, this aesthetic emphasizes quiet confidence, restorative balance, and grounded progression. It merges Scandinavian botanical minimalism with soft, tactile physical interaction models.

The visual language serves people seeking lifelong, habitual physical wellbeing rather than punitive metrics. The experience evokes the grounded serenity of an airy yoga sanctuary, morning mist over stone, and organic greenery. The design style pairs airy pastel background fields with generous, pillowy card surfaces, subtle organic pill tags, and tactile circular controls that honor mindful exercise cadences.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from botanical and earthy minerals, balancing pale sage backgrounds with deep slate typography for effortless legibility.

- **Primary (`#4A6B53` & `#35523E`):** Muted moss greens convey organic growth, stability, and grounded energy. Used for primary interactive actions, selected tabs, progress fills, and primary metrics.
- **Secondary (`#7B9E87`):** Soft sage accent used for secondary toggles, subtle active states, and calm completion rings.
- **Atmospheric Backgrounds (`#EBF1EC` & `#F4F7F4`):** Soft sage-tinted canvases that soothe eye fatigue and evoke morning natural light.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`#FFFFFF` & `#FAFCFA`):** Warm milk and soft off-white card layers that lift content gently from the pale sage background.
- **Functional Category Accents:**
  - **Strength & Vitality:** `#4A6B53` (Moss green)
  - **Mobility & Recovery:** `#8D84B3` (Soft gentle lavender / periwinkle)
  - **Cardio & Energy:** `#E08A68` (Warm muted coral / terracotta)
  - **Rest & Mindfulness:** `#648894` (Soft ocean slate)
- **Typography & Neutrals (`#1F2923` & `#2D3748`):** Deep forest charcoal and warm slate, replacing stark pure blacks with softer natural reading contrast. Secondary text uses `#6F7E74` for understated metadata.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides the core geometric clarity and gentle humanist curves required for a calm wellness companion. Rounded terminal endings and proportional numerals ensure fitness figures, timers, and repetition sets read instantly without visual clutter.

- **Display & Headlines:** Used for daily affirmations ("Movement is self-care"), active routine titles, and welcoming greeting headers. Tracked slightly tighter to provide cohesion.
- **Metrics & Numerals:** Workout stats, repetition rings, and weekly rhythm metrics use medium-bold weights to anchor attention without dominating cards.
- **Body & Labels:** Generous line heights preserve open whitespace and ensure effortless legibility during movement or active workout tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are designed primarily for mobile-first hand interaction, using relaxed edge margins (`1.25rem` / `20px`) and spacious stack groupings. Content breathes freely with generous interior card paddings (`1.25rem`). 

- **Vertical Cadence:** Multi-tier stacking prioritizes single-hand thumb navigation, with workout controls and primary actions positioned in the lower two-thirds of the viewport.
- **Modular Cards:** Dashboard components leverage split 2-column modular grids (e.g., Weekly Rhythm alongside Progress Sessions) with uniform `0.75rem` to `1rem` inter-card gaps.
- **Bottom Bar Safe Areas:** Fixed floating navigation bars sit elevated with generous padding, keeping thumbs clear of system home indicators while sustaining visual lightness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through soft, tonal atmospheric layering rather than harsh structural drops.

- **Base Field:** `#F4F7F4` (Muted pale sage canvas).
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Sheets):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or pale milk `#FAFCFA` surfaced atop sage fields, accompanied by ultra-diffused, botanical-tinted ambient shadows (`rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.04)` blur `20px`, spread `0px`, y-offset `6px`).
- **Floating Controls & Action Buttons:** Elevated with an additional soft ambient contact shadow (`rgba(74, 107, 83, 0.16)` blur `16px`, y-offset `6px`) to signal tactile affordance without harsh contrast borders.
- **Nested Wells & Inactive Indicators:** Inset backgrounds use `#EBF1EC` without borders, preserving smooth seamlessness across internal data structures and inactive weekly bars.

## Shapes

The design system adopts an organic, friendly, pillowy shape philosophy. Soft corners remove tension and stress from the workout experience.

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Use ultra-rounded radii (`1.5rem` to `1.75rem` / 24–28px) to soften rectangular mobile constraints.
- **Action Buttons & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create natural tactile zones for thumbs.
- **Chart Pillars & Metric Bars:** Caps on weekly rhythm graphs and set trackers are fully rounded cylinders, reinforcing botanical stems and organic growth.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Workout CTA:** Deep moss `#4A6B53` pill container with white typography, optional left-aligned leading icon, and gentle green-tinted shadow. Hover/pressed states deepen smoothly to `#35523E`.
- **Workout Player Controls:** Circular play/pause buttons (`64px` diameter) in deep moss `#4A6B53` with crisp white playback iconography, flanked by smaller circular back/skip pills (`48px`) in soft `#FFFFFF` with muted slate glyphs.
- **Ghost & Secondary Buttons:** Off-white or tinted sage container with `#1F2923` labels and `0.5px` tonal moss borders.

### Rhythm Bar Charts & Metric Rings
- **Weekly Rhythm Chart:** Vertical pill columns corresponding to Monday–Sunday. Active completed days show solid moss green gradient fills; current day shows an outlined or pulsed state; inactive future days rest in subtle `#EBF1EC` muted fills.
- **Progress Ring:** Minimalist circular stroke (`5px` thickness) tracking completed sessions versus target goals, using `#4A6B53` for the dynamic progress arc over an `#EBF1EC` track circle.

### Category & Activity Cards
- **Category Tiles (Strength, Mobility, Cardio, Recovery):** Soft pastel-tinted cards matching specific category accents (e.g., `#EBF1EC` for Strength, pale lavender `#F2EFF9` for Mobility, soft peach `#FDF2EE` for Cardio). Each tile houses a tonal rounded square icon badge, bold title, and gentle subtitle.
- **Hero Workout Card:** High-bleed photography or illustration on one side balanced with deep slate typography, pill difficulty tags ("Intermediate"), time duration badges, and direct action triggers.

### Navigation & Bottom Dock
- **Tab Bar:** Floating white capsule or edge-to-edge docked panel with 5 core destinations: Home, Workouts, Community, Progress, More. Active tabs illuminate in primary moss `#4A6B53` with an organic icon and text label; inactive tabs remain warm slate `#8C9B90`.
