Step-by-Step Form Cues

4 Key Phases
1
The Setup & Goblet Grip Solid base & vertical anchor
expand_more

Hold the dumbbell vertically directly against your sternum with both hands cupping the underside of the top weight plate. Set feet slightly outside shoulder-width, with toes flared outward 15 to 30 degrees to create natural hip clearance.

lightbulb Think: Glue the dumbbell to your chest like a sacred pendant.
2
The Descent (Eccentric 3s) Slow, controlled 3-second descent
expand_more

Inhale deeply into your 360-degree abdominal belt. Break at hips and knees simultaneously. Push your knees actively outward in line with your second toe while letting your elbows track inside your inner thighs.

compress Keep knees tracking outward; do not let arches collapse inward.
3
Bottom Pause (Isometric 1s) Full depth with proud posture
expand_more

Hit parallel or slightly below femur-crease depth. Hold tension for one clear second without relaxing or 'bouncing' out of the hole. Maintain an upright, proud chest and neutral lumbar spine.

4
The Drive (Concentric 2s) Powerful push through midfoot & heels
expand_more

Exhale with deliberate pressure through your midfoot and heels. Drive hips up smoothly to the standing start. Squeeze glutes firmly at the top without leaning backwards or hyperextending your lower back.

healing

Mindful Adjustments

close Elbows flaring out wide like wings

Gentle Fix: Tuck elbows tight into your ribcage. This stabilizes your thoracic spine and gives your knees an open corridor to track freely inside.

close Heels rising off the mat

Gentle Fix: Widen stance by 2 inches or elevate heels slightly on a 5lb plate while ankle dorsiflexion mobility develops.

spa

The Welora Philosophy

Depth is personal. Never force range of motion that induces lower back rounding. A smooth 90-degree squat with calm breathing honors your body far more than an uncontrolled deep drop.
